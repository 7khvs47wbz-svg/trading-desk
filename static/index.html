Getting started

  1. Check the market. This page says whether it's a good time to take new risk (Posture and Exposure ceiling).
  2. Find ideas. In Screener, run a screen, and press “How has this screen done?” to see its 5-year track record.
  3. Watch them. Star a stock to add it to your Watchlist, and click any ticker to open its chart.
  4. Practise. “Paper trade” sizes the position from your stop and runs the discipline check. It's $100,000 of pretend money.
  5. Learn. Close trades and the Journal shows what's working: by setup, by plan, by R.
